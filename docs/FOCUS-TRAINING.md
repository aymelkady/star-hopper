# Focus games in Star Hopper (for parents)

Star Hopper has a few small attention games mixed into the adventure. They are short, they are
optional, and they can't be lost.

## The Focus Sun (meter)

The little sun next to the goal card at the top fills up while your child works toward the
**current goal**: walking toward it, picking up the items it needs, winning a focus game, finding a
star. When the sun is full it turns into a **Focus Burst**: a 10-second golden trail and double
coins, then it starts again from zero.

If your child wanders for more than 25 seconds without getting closer to the goal, the sun slowly
gets dimmer. That's all that happens. It never turns red, never shows a message, and nothing is
taken away. Three bursts in one session get a cheerful "3 bursts today!". Nothing is counted
across days (no streaks).

## The games

| Game | Where | What your child does | What it practises |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💎 **Gem Rush** (×3) | Near each zone entrance (meadow path, cove beach, towers runway) | Gems pop up one at a time. Grab the blue ones and leave the red ones. | **Stopping an automatic response** (inhibition, "Go / No-Go") |
| ❄️ **Freeze!** | Anywhere, 2–3 times per session, 3–6 min apart | A whistle, then a snowflake card: stand still for 2 seconds. | **Impulse control**: stopping on cue |
| 🏮 **Two Things** | Splash Cove log ride | Ride the logs and jump only when the lantern is green. | **Divided attention**: watching two things at once |
| 🔍 **Spot It** | Crate Isle, Sky Towers (plank from the hub) | Find the 3 crates with a tiny star and ground-pound them (60 s). A soft ping gets faster when the camera looks at a star crate. | **Sustained attention and visual search** |
| 🎐 **Echo Chimes** | Sky Towers chimes | Ring the chimes in the right order. | **Working memory** |

Each game starts on a coloured ring on the ground. A card shows the one rule (no more than 5
words, with an icon) and waits for a tap, then counts 3-2-1. Every run takes under 90 seconds.
Walking away from the ring just closes the card.

## Why it's optional and "can't be lost"

- Each game gives one **bonus star** (5 in all, so the total is 20 instead of 15). The goal card
  suggests them after the second star of their zone, and today's quest has at most one. They can be
  skipped like any other goal.
- There are no lives and no penalties. A wrong answer plays a soft "oops" and costs nothing.
  Freeze! never takes coins away.
- After a miss, the result card offers **Try again** or **Easier**. After two misses in a row,
  **Easier** becomes the main button.
- Freeze! can be switched off in Settings ("Freeze! game").

## How the difficulty adapts

For each game type, the game keeps the **last 10 answers** for that save slot (right or wrong,
plus reaction time) and picks one of **5 levels**:

- **Up a level** after a run where at least 85% of the last 10 were right, with fast answers
  (median under 1.2 s).
- **Down a level** when fewer than 60% were right.
- Higher levels mean shorter gems (1.6 s → 0.9 s), more red decoys (20% → 40%), shorter green
  lanterns (1.8 s → 1.0 s), and smaller star marks.

Levels, recent answers, burst count and results are saved with the slot (`focus` in the save).

## An honest note

These games borrow ideas from published attention-training research: go/no-go, dual-task and
visual-search tasks, plus adaptive difficulty. They're designed to make focusing feel like play,
with short goals, clear feedback and no punishment.

They are **design-informed, not clinically validated**. Star Hopper is a game, not a treatment.
It hasn't been tested in a study and makes no medical claims. If you have questions about your
child's attention, please talk to your child's doctor or specialist.
